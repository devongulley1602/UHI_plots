Static Fields
Static geophysical fields in the limited area domain (LAD). Urban fraction is supplied by the model, and point selections are made on applying a 1.5-cell standard deviation Gaussian blur, in this case to urban fraction for station points. Three categories of (blurred) urban fraction for each station gridpoint is as follows - Rural: [0,1%) Suburban [1%,50%], Urban: (50%,100%]. "MTL stations" have data availability and are near the city in a very limited area domain (VLAD, bounded by 45N-46N,72.58W-74.58W).

Station Characteristics
Data availability and visualisation for the ECCC 3rd Generation Homogenised Temperature Data for all LAD stations from 1980-2022 inclusive. "Obs" is the final station subset of MTL stations created by removing all stations having too different elevations from urban areas (elevations >2 urban standard deviations from the urban mean). A Kernel Density Estimation was used for drawing continuous distributions.

Summer Climatological Anomalies by Year (Preliminary)
ECCC 3rd generation homogenised station temperature averages of each year's summer (JJA) between 1980-2022 for selected stations in the LAD with sufficient data availability. Anomalies were defined with respect to the average 1980-2010 summer.
