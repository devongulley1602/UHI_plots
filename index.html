Goal: Evaluate Montréal's urban heat island (UHI) according to CRCM6 output, observations, and the impact of integrating TEB into the surface scheme.

Full fields and unfiltered stations in the LAD
  • Static CLASS and TEB static geophysical fields in the 1km grid-cell size limited area domain (LAD).
  • CLASS and TEB+CLASS refer to the two different surface schemes in the CRCM6 LAD output, the latter with the more sophisticated urban surface representation.
  • Data availability and visualisation for the ECCC 3rd Generation Homogenised Temperature Data for all LAD stations from 1980-2022 inclusive.

Station characteristics, classification, and filtering confounding factors
  • The convention for extracting all model point-values is to subject the modelled field to a 1.5-cell standard deviation Gaussian blur.
  • Urban, suburban, and rural obs stations reside in a model cell with the (blurred) furban CLASS vegetation field of (0%, 0.1%), [0.1%,50%], and (50,100%) respectively.
  • The (blurred) water fraction (i.e. inland lake) is selected to be <20% and stations with weak data availability from 2000-2022 are removed.
  • "Obs" is the final subset of stations in the LAD nearest Montréal with some additional filtering of elevations >2 urban standard deviations from the urban mean.
  • A Kernel Density Estimation was used for drawing continuous distributions to illustrate the filtering steps used to select obs.

Climatology of obs and simobs
  • Full seasonal climatology 1980-2023 of obs stations for daily temperature data grouped by year.
  • Anomalies are defined with respect to the average 1980-2010 by station type (urban, rural suburban).
  • "Simobs" seasonal averages are determined by taking a given model's seasonal average, blurring the resulting field and taking the value of the cell corresponding to the obs station.
  • Difference between seasonal simobs and obs gives a model (CLASS or TEB+CLASS) accuracy by season.
  • Unless otherwise noted, obs and simobs refers to these stations in the 2000-2022 time slice constrained by the years that were simulated.
